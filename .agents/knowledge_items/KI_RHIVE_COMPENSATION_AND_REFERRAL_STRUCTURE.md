## 👔 1. Project Design Specialist (PDS) Commission Architecture
- **Contract Type:** 1099 Independent Contractor.
- **Lead Origination Channels:**
  - Canvassing App: `https://app.rhiveconstruction.com/canvass`
  - Referral Entry Portal: `https://app.rhiveconstruction.com/referrals`
  - QOS Pipeline Desk: `https://app.rhiveconstruction.com`
- **Deposit Requirement:** Client pays **50% Initial Investment Deposit** at Stage 4 contract signing.
- **Commission Formula:**
  - $\text{Gross Profit (GP)} = \text{Contract Value} - \text{Direct Costs (~80%)}$
  - $\text{Company Retention} = \$1,000 \text{ (first \$50k)} + 2\% \text{ (\$50k-\$150k)} + 3\% \text{ (above \$150k)}$
  - $\text{CGP} = \text{GP} - \text{Company Retention}$
- **Performance Tiers (% of CGP):**
  - **Scout:** `30% CGP` *(Initial Goal: 4 deals / $50k+ in 60 days. Advanced Scout Goal: 40 deals / $500k+ volume = $20,000 net payout)*
  - **Builder:** `40% CGP` *($2M volume / 50 projects = $24,000 payout on 40 deals)*
  - **Lead:** `50% CGP` *(+ 10%-20% CGP Override on mentored contractors)*
  - **Mentor:** `60% CGP` *(+ 10%-30% Regional CGP Override)*
- **Payment Schedule:**
  - **First Check (At 50% Deposit):** `50% of estimated commission` (`(Tier % × Estimated CGP) × 0.50`).
  - **Final Check (At Project Completion & Collection):** Remaining balance adjusted for actual final CGP upon deposit clearance (`Balance == $0`).
- **Mid-Project Departure Clause:** Commissions are paid strictly for active services rendered while engaged at RHIVE. If a PDS leaves, quits, or is terminated prior to final walkthrough and billing, **they retain only the milestone draws earned and paid during active employment** and forfeit unearned final payouts. If a PDS originated a lead without quoting/managing, they earn the **$350.00 referral fee**.

## 🌟 2. RHIVE Abundance Network (Group & Fundraiser Profit Share)
- **Target Partners:** High school sports teams, clubs, academic groups, police/fire stations, and civic non-profits.
- **Value Prop:** Zero upfront costs, no door-to-door selling, zero administrative burden for school staff.
- **Tiered Calendar Year Profit Share (Resets Jan 1st):**
  - **Tier 1 (Community Partner):** Roofs 1–5 in calendar year -> `30% CGP`
  - **Tier 2 (Impact Champion):** Roofs 6–10 in calendar year -> `40% CGP`
  - **Tier 3 (Abundance Leader / Legacy Partner):** 11+ Roofs in calendar year -> `50% CGP`
  - **Annual Reset:** Tier counter resets to 0 on **January 1st** of each year.
- **Language Rule:** Always specify roof sizes in **square footage (e.g. 20 SQ (2,000 sq ft) Roof)** when addressing community leaders.
- **Tech Features:** Ghost QR Code & Share Link, live "Pizza Tracker" thermometer dashboard.
- **Payout Trigger:** Unlocks automatically upon final investment deposit verification (`Balance == $0`).

## 🤝 3. Individual Referral Program
- **Referral Fee:** **`$350.00`** flat payout per lead closed within **6 Months (180 Days)**.
- **Strict 1st-Person Basis:** Advocate inputs address via Geocoded Address Selector Tool + Referred Prospect First Name, Last Name, Phone, and Email. Prospect MUST be aware of the referral when RHIVE calls.
- **Payout Trigger:** Unlocks upon final investment deposit verification (`Balance == $0`).

## 📑 4. IRS W-9 & Tax Compliance Matrix
- **PDS (1099 Contractor):** W-9 required prior to 1st draw/commission payout. Form 1099-NEC.
- **Abundance Fundraiser / Group:** W-9 required prior to reaching $600 cumulative annual earnings. Form 1099-MISC/NEC.
- **Individual Referrers ($350):** 1st payout processed immediately upon deposit. W-9 required prior to receiving 2nd referral payout (when reaching/exceeding $600). Form 1099-NEC.
