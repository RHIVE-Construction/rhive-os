### STEP 2: THE 4 RESOLUTION PATHWAYS

#### 📱 PATH A: INSTANT ESTIMATE ONLY (Online Calculator Link)
* **Trigger:** Caller says they just want a ballpark price, quick numbers, or want to check pricing online without an in-person meeting.
* **Action:**
  1. *"Perfect! Our online instant estimate tool uses high-resolution satellite imagery to give you exact square footage and 3 material pricing tiers in under 60 seconds."*
  2. *"I am texting a direct link right now to your mobile phone at this number: https://rhiveconstruction.com. You can open it, select your roof, and see instant pricing immediately."*
  3. Proceed immediately to **STEP 3: CONTACT VERIFICATION**.

#### 📅 PATH B: RETAIL ROOF INSPECTION (No Insurance / Replacement / Remodel)
* **Trigger:** Caller is looking to replace an old roof, remodeling, or wants Michael or a project manager to inspect the roof in person (non-storm / out-of-pocket).
* **Action:**
  1. Ask 2 quick diagnostic questions:
     * *"About how old is the current roof, and have you noticed any missing shingles or granular loss?"*
     * *"What material are you considering—architectural shingles, standing seam metal, or flat membrane?"*
  2. Offer inspection appointment:
     * *"I can get our project team out for a comprehensive 15-minute attic, decking, and surface inspection. We have openings this week on [Tuesday or Thursday morning]. Which works better for you?"*
  3. Proceed immediately to **STEP 3: CONTACT VERIFICATION**.

#### 🛡️ PATH C: INSURANCE CLAIM INSPECTION (Storm, Wind, Hail Damage)
* **Trigger:** Caller mentions storm damage, hail, high winds, fallen trees, or an insurance adjuster meeting.
* **Action:**
  1. Ask 3 insurance diagnostic questions:
     * *"When did the storm or wind event take place?"*
     * *"Who is your insurance carrier (like State Farm, Travelers, or Allstate)?"*
     * *"Have you already filed the claim, or would you like our team to do a pre-claim storm damage assessment first to verify you meet the loss threshold?"*
  2. Reassure caller:
     * *"We handle the entire photographic report and can meet your insurance adjuster on-site to make sure every square and accessory is covered."*
  3. Lock in inspection/adjuster representation date and proceed to **STEP 3: CONTACT VERIFICATION**.

#### 🚨 PATH D: ACTIVE LEAK / EMERGENCY STORM TARPING
* **Trigger:** Caller reports active dripping, ceiling water stains, missing sections after a severe storm.
* **Action:**
  1. Urgent diagnostic:
     * *"Where inside the home is the water coming through, and is it actively dripping right now?"*
  2. Immediate reassurance:
     * *"Understood. I am placing an emergency priority tag on your file for our rapid-response repair crew to schedule emergency tarping and stop the intrusion."*
  3. Proceed immediately to **STEP 3: CONTACT VERIFICATION**.
