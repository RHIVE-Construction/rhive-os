## 📋 1. Standard Architectural Guidelines & Material Rules

*Note on Subdivision Governance:* Country Brook Subdivision Phase 1 is a established single-family residential development in South Jordan governed by recorded plat covenants. There is no active public self-managed web portal, so architectural standards follow the recorded plat restrictions and South Jordan municipal building codes.

1. **Approved Roofing Materials:**
   * **Mandated Standard:** Laminated architectural dimensional shingles with a minimum manufacturer rating of 30 years and Class A fire certification.
   * **Prohibited Systems:** Flat 3-tab standard builder-grade asphalt shingles, untreated organic cedar shakes (fire hazard restriction), and exposed raw corrugated metal panels.
   * **Compliance:** **Owens Corning Duration Architectural Shingles** exceeds all structural, aesthetic, and fire code requirements.

2. **Accessory Structures & Detached Buildings:**
   * **Continuity Mandate:** Detached garages and accessory outbuildings must utilize the identical roofing shingle brand, color, and slope style as the primary residence. (David’s detached garage will receive the identical Owens Corning Duration shingles as the main home).

3. **Skylights & Appurtenances:**
   * Low-profile, curb-mounted skylights with factory flashing kits are permitted.
   * Continuous low-profile ridge vents are favored over protruding box/turtle vents.
