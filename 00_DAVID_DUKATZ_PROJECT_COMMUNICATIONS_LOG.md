## 💧 7. South Side Window Well Drainage Case Study (Gutter Solution)

**The Problem:** Heavy rains cause water pooling along the South side where two basement window wells are currently unprotected by gutters.  
* **Risk:** South Jordan soil contains clay layers. Uncontrolled roof runoff dumping directly from an un-guttered eave creates hydraulic pressure against foundation walls, leading to window well flooding and basement wall seepage.
* **The RHIVE Solution:**
  1. Install a continuous run of **5" K-Style Seamless Gutter** along the entire South eave (184 LF total perimeter).
  2. Anchor with **hidden screw hangers** every 24 inches into solid sub-fascia.
  3. Route the South downspout past the basement window wells with a **4-foot hinged ground extension** directing all runoff into the natural lawn drainage gradient.
