## 🚫 SCENARIO 13: Unsolicited Third-Party Marketing / SEO Sales Pitch (Spam Rejection)
* **Caller Persona:** Dominant / Pushy Sales Rep — Trying to sell Google Ranking, Lead Generation, or Marketing services.
* **Caller Profile:** Alex from "Roofing SEO Leads Agency".
* **Testing Goal:** Identify unsolicited marketing/sales pitch $\rightarrow$ Politely decline $\rightarrow$ **Hang up immediately**.

```text
AI VOICEBOT (Hunni): "Thanks for calling R-HIVE Construction, Roofing Specialists. I'm Hunni. Who do I have the pleasure of speaking with?"

YOUR SCRIPTED VOICE INPUT (Turn 1):
"Hi Hunni, my name is Alex."

EXPECTED AI RESPONSE (Turn 1):
"Hi Alex! Are you a new or existing customer today?"

YOUR SCRIPTED VOICE INPUT (Turn 2):
"Actually, I'm calling from Roofing SEO Leads. We help roofing contractors in Utah get 50 new qualified homeowner leads every month through Google Ads."

EXPECTED AI RESPONSE (Turn 2):
"Thanks for calling R-HIVE, but we do not accept unsolicited marketing or sales calls. Have a great day!"
[SYSTEM TRIGGER: DISCONNECT / HANG UP CALL IMMEDIATELY]
```

## 🟢 SCENARIO 4: Recent Repair <15 Yrs Old WITH Photos (Influential DISC)
* **Caller Persona:** Influential (I) — Enthusiastic, talkative.
* **Caller Profile:** Brandon (Homeowner with a 6-year-old roof leak).
* **Testing Goal:** Repair <15 Yrs + Photos $\rightarrow$ **Address Verification** $\rightarrow$ **Path 1 Instant Photo SMS Hook from Michael**.

```text
AI VOICEBOT (Hunni): "Thanks for calling R-HIVE Construction, Roofing Specialists. I'm Hunni. Who do I have the pleasure of speaking with?"

YOUR SCRIPTED VOICE INPUT (Turn 1):
"Hi Hunni! My name is Brandon."

EXPECTED AI RESPONSE (Turn 1):
"Hi Brandon! Are you a new or existing customer today?"

YOUR SCRIPTED VOICE INPUT (Turn 2):
"I'm a new customer needing a leak repair quote."

EXPECTED AI RESPONSE (Turn 2):
"Hi Brandon! What is the address of the home or building you're calling about?"

YOUR SCRIPTED VOICE INPUT (Turn 3):
"742 Evergreen Terrace in Sandy."

EXPECTED AI RESPONSE (Turn 3):
"Let me verify that for you, you said 7-4-2, E-V-E-R-G-R-E-E-N Terrace, and that's in Sandy?"

YOUR SCRIPTED VOICE INPUT (Turn 4):
"Yes, perfect!"

EXPECTED AI RESPONSE (Turn 4):
"Thanks Brandon! What are you noticing on the roof? About how old is it?"

YOUR SCRIPTED VOICE INPUT (Turn 5):
"Small chimney leak, roof is only 6 years old, and I have photos!"

EXPECTED AI RESPONSE (Turn 5):
"Gathering details now... I'm sending an instant text from Michael to your phone right this second. Reply with photos of the leak, and we'll sync them to your file!"
[SYSTEM TRIGGER: INSTANT SMS HOOK FROM MICHAEL + CRM SYNC]
```
