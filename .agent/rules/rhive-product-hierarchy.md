# RHIVE MASTER PRODUCT & PROPOSAL HIERARCHY

## Shingle Classification & Terminology Standards
Whenever drafting, reviewing, or editing roofing packages, estimates, scopes of work, or proposal copy for RHIVE Construction:

1. **Standard Architectural:** Owens Corning **Oakridge®** (Baseline dimensional shingle).
2. **Premium Architectural:** Owens Corning **Duration®** (Upgraded baseline featuring SureNail® Technology & TruDefinition® Colors).
3. **Class 4 Impact Architectural:** Owens Corning **Duration FLEX®** (SBS Polymer-Modified for extreme hail defense & 20-30% insurance premium discounts).
4. **Designer Shake Line:** Owens Corning **Woodcrest®** (Thick, rustic craftsman wood-shake aesthetics). Upgrade labeled as **Designer Shake Upgrade** or **Craftsman Wood-Shake Upgrade**.
5. **Premium Designer Shake Line:** Owens Corning **Woodmoor®** (Extra-heavyweight multi-ply luxury estate shake). Upgrade labeled as **Premium Designer Heavyweight Shake Upgrade** or **Estate Luxury Shake Upgrade**.

## Absolute Prohibitions:
- **BANNED:** Calling Woodcrest or Woodmoor "architectural shingles" or "architectural upgrades".
- **BANNED:** Calling standard Duration a "standard shingle" without noting it as the Premium Architectural baseline over Oakridge.
