Frequently Asked Questions & Pricing Standards

How much does a roof replacement cost in Utah?

Residential roof replacement in Utah typically ranges between $8,500 and $22,000 depending on square footage, pitch, layer count, and material selection. RHIVE provides 100% itemized pricing separating materials, labor, and profit margins.

What is the RHIVE Project Savings Promotion (RPSP)?

The RPSP is an efficiency credit that eliminates contractor follow-up chase costs. Homeowners who approve their bid within 48 hours receive an instant 10% credit (up to $1,000) applied directly to their project total.

What is RHIVE's 100 sq ft OSB decking standard?

RHIVE includes the replacement of up to 100 square feet (up to 3 full 4x8 sheets) of deteriorated or rotten OSB/plywood roof decking at $0 additional charge in every residential full tear-off contract.

What is the difference between commercial TPO and PVC flat roofing?

TPO delivers superior thermal reflection and UV resistance for standard commercial facilities. PVC delivers superior chemical and animal fat resistance, making it essential for restaurants and facilities with rooftop ventilation exhaust.
